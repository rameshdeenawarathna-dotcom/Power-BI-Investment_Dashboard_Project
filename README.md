# Financial Market Investment Valuation Dashboard
Project is a Power BI–based financial market investment valuation and analytics dashboard designed to support timely valuation, monitoring, and analysis of fixed-income investment portfolios.The solution integrates Power BI, Power Query, and SQL to transform financial market and investment data into meaningful insights for finance and investment.

## Project Overview

This project is a **Power BI–based financial market investment valuation and analytics dashboard** designed to support timely valuation, monitoring, and analysis of fixed-income investment portfolios.

The solution integrates **Power BI, Power Query, and SQL** to transform financial market and investment data into meaningful insights for finance and investment decision-making.

The dashboard covers multiple financial instruments, including:

1.Treasury Bonds
2.Treasury Bills
3.Commercial Papers
4.Corporate Debentures

## Key Features

The dashboard provides a centralized view of the investment portfolio, enabling users to monitor **market value, investment performance, maturity profiles, yields, interest income, and portfolio composition**.

Key analytical capabilities include:

* Timely valuation of fixed-income securities
* Portfolio market value and carrying value analysis
* Yield and interest rate analysis
* Accrued interest calculations
* Coupon and interest income monitoring
* Maturity analysis and identification of upcoming maturities
* Portfolio allocation by financial instrument
* Weighted-average portfolio measures
* Investment performance monitoring
* Interactive filtering and drill-down analysis
* Automated data transformation to reduce manual processing

## Technology Used

**Power BI** – Data modelling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards, KPIs and financial visualisations

**Power Query** – Data extraction, cleansing, transformation and preparation

**SQL** – Data extraction, querying and preparation of financial datasets

## Business Value

## Business Value

The Financial Market Investment Valuation Dashboard transforms complex investment data into a centralized and interactive reporting solution, providing finance and investment teams with timely visibility into the **valuation, income generation, maturity profile, and economic value of fixed-income investments**.

The dashboard supports more effective investment portfolio management through the following capabilities:

* **Timely Investment Valuation:** Provides updated valuations for Treasury Bonds, Treasury Bills, Commercial Papers, and Corporate Debentures, enabling users to monitor the current value of the investment portfolio and assess changes in value over time.

* **Coupon Monitoring:** Tracks coupon rates, coupon payment dates, accrued interest, and expected coupon income across interest-bearing securities. This helps finance and investment teams monitor upcoming cash inflows and identify securities with approaching coupon payment dates.

* **Maturity Monitoring:** Provides visibility into upcoming maturities of Treasury Bills, Treasury Bonds, Commercial Papers, and Debentures. This enables management to anticipate investment cash flows, plan reinvestment activities, and reduce the risk of missing maturity-related actions.

* **Economic Value Analysis:** Supports assessment of the underlying economic value of investments by combining key information such as market value, yields, coupon rates, maturity dates, accrued interest, and investment cash flows. This provides management with a clearer understanding of how market movements and investment characteristics affect portfolio value.

* **Yield and Return Analysis:** Enables comparison of investment yields across different instruments, issuers, and maturity periods, helping decision-makers evaluate portfolio performance and identify potential reinvestment opportunities.

* **Portfolio Composition Analysis:** Provides an interactive view of portfolio allocation across different financial instruments, issuers, maturity periods, and other relevant categories, supporting diversification and concentration analysis.

* **Cash Flow and Liquidity Planning:** Visibility into coupon payments, interest receipts, and investment maturities allows finance teams to anticipate future cash inflows and incorporate them into liquidity and treasury planning.

* **Reinvestment Decision Support:** By identifying upcoming maturities and providing relevant yield and valuation information, the dashboard enables investment teams to plan reinvestment decisions proactively rather than relying on manual maturity tracking.

* **Improved Management Decision-Making:** Interactive Power BI visualisations allow senior management and investment teams to quickly identify significant portfolio movements, upcoming maturities, income opportunities, and areas requiring further analysis.

* **Reduced Manual Processing:** Power Query and SQL automate significant portions of data extraction, cleansing, transformation, and preparation, reducing dependence on manual spreadsheets and repetitive calculations.

* **Improved Data Accuracy and Consistency:** A structured data model and automated transformation process provide a consistent basis for investment valuation and reporting, reducing the risk of manual errors and improving the reliability of management information.

### Overall Business Impact

The dashboard converts investment data into **actionable financial intelligence**, allowing management to move beyond static investment reports toward proactive portfolio monitoring. By bringing together **valuations, yields, coupons, maturities, accrued income, cash flows, and economic value** within a single analytical environment, the solution supports better investment decisions, stronger liquidity planning, timely reinvestment decisions, and more efficient financial reporting.
