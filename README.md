# Financial Market Investment Valuation Dashboard
Project is a Power BI–based financial market investment valuation and analytics dashboard designed to support timely valuation, monitoring, and analysis of fixed-income investment portfolios.The solution integrates Power BI, Power Query, and SQL to transform financial market and investment data into meaningful insights for finance and investment.

## Project Overview

This project is a **Power BI–based financial market investment valuation and analytics dashboard** designed to support timely valuation, monitoring, and analysis of fixed-income investment portfolios.

The solution integrates **Power BI, Power Query, and SQL** to transform financial market and investment data into meaningful insights for finance and investment decision-making.

The dashboard covers multiple financial instruments, including:

1.Treasury Bonds
2.Treasury Bills
3.Commercial Papers
4.Corporate Debentures

## Key Features

The dashboard provides a centralized view of the investment portfolio, enabling users to monitor **market value, investment performance, maturity profiles, yields, interest income, and portfolio composition**.

Key analytical capabilities include:

* Timely valuation of fixed-income securities
* Portfolio market value and carrying value analysis
* Yield and interest rate analysis
* Accrued interest calculations
* Coupon and interest income monitoring
* Maturity analysis and identification of upcoming maturities
* Portfolio allocation by financial instrument
* Weighted-average portfolio measures
* Investment performance monitoring
* Interactive filtering and drill-down analysis
* Automated data transformation to reduce manual processing

## Technology Used

**Power BI** – Data modelling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards, KPIs and financial visualisations

**Power Query** – Data extraction, cleansing, transformation and preparation

**SQL** – Data extraction, querying and preparation of financial datasets

## Economic & Financial Market Outlook

In addition to fixed-income investment valuation and portfolio monitoring, the dashboard incorporates an **Economic and Financial Market Outlook** to provide decision-makers with a broader understanding of the macroeconomic and capital-market environment.

The objective is to connect investment portfolio analysis with key economic and financial-market indicators, enabling users to understand not only the performance and value of individual investments but also the wider economic conditions that may influence **interest rates, investment yields, liquidity, market valuations, and investment decisions**.

### Export Turnover Analysis

The dashboard provides year-by-year analysis of **export earnings and trends**, allowing users to monitor the country's external trade performance.

Historical trend analysis helps identify periods of export growth or contraction and provides additional context when assessing foreign-exchange inflows, economic activity, and overall external-sector performance.

### Import Turnover Analysis

Import turnover is analysed on a year-by-year basis to provide visibility into changes in the country's demand for imported goods and services.

Comparing import and export trends provides useful insights into **trade performance, foreign-exchange requirements, and broader economic activity**.

### Workers' Remittances

The dashboard tracks **workers' remittance inflows over time**, providing year-on-year visibility into an important source of foreign-currency inflows.

Trend analysis enables users to identify changes in remittance income and understand its contribution to the external sector and foreign-exchange liquidity.

### Tourism Revenue

Tourism earnings are incorporated into the economic outlook to monitor the **year-by-year contribution of tourism-related foreign-currency inflows**.

The dashboard enables users to identify recovery, growth, or contraction trends in tourism revenue and consider these developments alongside other external-sector indicators.

### GDP Analysis

The dashboard presents **Gross Domestic Product (GDP)** information and historical trends to provide an overall view of economic activity.

GDP information allows investment and finance professionals to assess broader economic conditions and place financial-market developments within the context of economic expansion or contraction.

### Per Capita Income

Historical **per capita income** is presented to provide an additional perspective on economic development and income levels over time.

Combining per capita income with GDP and other macroeconomic indicators provides users with a broader understanding of long-term economic trends rather than relying solely on aggregate economic output.

### Primary Market Information

The dashboard incorporates **primary market information** relating to government and other fixed-income securities.

This provides visibility into relevant market information such as:

* Treasury Bill and Treasury Bond issuances
* Auction information
* Market yields
* Maturity periods
* Interest and coupon rates
* Issuance volumes
* Changes in primary-market conditions

Integrating primary-market information with the investment portfolio allows users to compare existing investments against prevailing market conditions and supports **valuation, yield analysis, maturity planning, and reinvestment decisions**.

### Stock Exchange & Capital Market Analysis

The dashboard also incorporates **stock exchange information** to provide a broader view of capital-market conditions.

The analysis can include market indices, market turnover, trading activity, market capitalisation and historical market movements, allowing users to monitor trends in equity-market activity alongside fixed-income and macroeconomic indicators.

This provides investment professionals with a more comprehensive view of the financial market rather than analysing fixed-income securities in isolation.

## Integrated Economic & Investment Intelligence

A key strength of the dashboard is the integration of **investment portfolio information and macroeconomic indicators within a single Business Intelligence environment**.

Users can monitor:

**Investment Portfolio:** Treasury Bonds, Treasury Bills, Commercial Papers and Debentures

**Investment Income:** Coupons, accrued interest, yields and investment income

**Investment Lifecycle:** Purchase dates, coupon dates, maturity dates and upcoming cash flows

**Valuation:** Market values, portfolio values and relevant economic values

**External Sector:** Exports, imports, workers' remittances and tourism earnings

**Economic Performance:** GDP and per capita income

**Financial Markets:** Primary-market yields, government security information and stock-market indicators

This integrated approach enables finance and investment professionals to move from simply reporting historical investment balances toward understanding the **economic and market environment surrounding investment decisions**.

## Business Value

The solution creates a centralized **financial market intelligence and investment analytics platform** by combining portfolio-level data with economic and capital-market information.

It supports management in monitoring upcoming **coupon receipts and investment maturities**, evaluating current portfolio valuations, planning future cash flows, identifying reinvestment requirements, comparing portfolio yields against prevailing market conditions, and understanding changes in the broader economic environment.

The inclusion of GDP, per capita income, exports, imports, workers' remittances, tourism revenue, primary-market information and stock-exchange indicators provides additional context for interpreting movements in **interest rates, market yields, liquidity conditions and investment valuations**.

By integrating **Power BI, Power Query and SQL**, the solution also reduces reliance on manually prepared spreadsheets and consolidates multiple financial and economic datasets into an interactive analytical environment.

Overall, the dashboard demonstrates how **financial expertise and Business Intelligence can be combined to transform investment and economic data into actionable management information**, supporting more timely, informed and data-driven financial decision-making.
